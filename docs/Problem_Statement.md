# Problem Statement

Hospitals, especially Intensive Care Units (ICUs), emergency departments, and general wards, are responsible for monitoring a large number of patients simultaneously. Due to high patient-to-staff ratios and continuous clinical workload, it is challenging for doctors and nurses to observe every patient at all times.

As a result, early signs of patient deterioration may go unnoticed, leading to delayed medical intervention and potentially affecting patient outcomes.

Although hospitals use monitoring devices such as ECG, pulse oximeters, and blood pressure monitors, continuous visual assessment of patient behavior still relies heavily on healthcare professionals. Important visual indicators such as facial distress, abnormal body posture, prolonged inactivity, or unusual movements may not always receive immediate attention.

This project proposes **MedGuardian AI**, an AI-assisted patient monitoring and early warning system that continuously analyzes visual cues from hospitalized patients using Computer Vision and Machine Learning.

The system estimates patient deterioration risk based on multiple visual indicators, assigns an appropriate risk level, and provides intelligent multi-level alerts to healthcare professionals. The objective is to support clinical decision-making by helping medical staff identify patients who may require timely assessment.

MedGuardian AI is designed as a clinical decision-support system. It does not diagnose diseases or replace doctors. Instead, it assists healthcare professionals by providing continuous monitoring, explainable risk assessments, and timely notifications.
