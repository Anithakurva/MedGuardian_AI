# MedGuardian AI

## Functional Requirements

### FR-01
The system shall continuously monitor patients using live hospital camera feeds.

### FR-02
The system shall detect every patient present in the camera frame.

### FR-03
The system shall uniquely identify and track each patient throughout the monitoring session.

### FR-04
The system shall analyze facial expressions to estimate pain or distress indicators.

### FR-05
The system shall monitor eye status and detect prolonged eye closure or reduced responsiveness.

### FR-06
The system shall analyze body posture and detect abnormal posture changes.

### FR-07
The system shall continuously monitor patient movement and inactivity.

### FR-08
The system shall combine multiple visual features to estimate patient deterioration risk.

### FR-09
The system shall classify patients into five risk levels.

- Stable
- Observation
- Moderate
- High Risk
- Critical

### FR-10
The system shall generate intelligent alerts based on configurable confidence thresholds.

### FR-11
The system shall provide explainable reasons behind every risk prediction.

### FR-12
The system shall display live patient information on the hospital dashboard.

### FR-13
The system shall maintain complete monitoring history for every patient.

### FR-14
The system shall prioritize alerts according to patient severity.

### FR-15
The system shall support real-time monitoring with minimum delay.
